# Extended differences

[English](extend-features.md) · [한국어](extend-features-ko.md)

[Back to README](../README.md) · [Development guide](development.md)

Extended remains a separate edition for future gameplay, balance, and content development. It is currently for local development; CI builds and releases are disabled.

The five [QOL options](qol.md) are shared with Stranded II; they are no longer exclusive features. They default to **OFF in Stranded II** and **ON in Extended**, with existing selections preserved. Both editions use the original crafting, weight, and construction rules. No Extended-only gameplay or content is currently added.

Executables, versions, resources, settings, and saves remain separate. Data is not automatically copied between editions.
