# Extended development ideas

[English](plan.md) · [한국어](plan-ko.md)

[Current edition differences](../extend-features.md) · [Studio](../studio.md) · [Development guide](../development.md)

Extended is currently for local development and has no exclusive gameplay or content yet. The following are development ideas, not confirmed or completed features. The five QOL options are already shared with the base game, with different defaults for each edition.

## Scenarios and compatibility

Future balance and content changes belong in Extended-specific modules and resources. Depending on their scope, they may require separate scenarios or conversion of existing maps and saves; compatibility policy will be decided when designing those features. This does not mean the original scenarios are currently incompatible.

## Systems to explore

| Idea | Current scope |
| --- | --- |
| Food spoilage | A candidate for new gameplay rules. |
| Terrain editing during play | A gameplay expansion idea, separate from Studio's existing terrain editing. |
| Housing | A separate system extending existing construction has not been designed or implemented. |
| Large world | Explore a 2048×2048 world with chunks loaded and unloaded as the player moves. Chunk streaming is not implemented, and performance improvements have not been verified. |
